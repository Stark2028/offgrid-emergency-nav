Each generation's manifest records its source snapshot timestamp and SHA-256.
The corresponding compressed source extracts are distributed in this repository
under packages/pipeline/snapshots/. The application displays attribution on
every coverage map. OFFGRID's MIT code license does not replace the data license.

These bounded starter extracts are not full-city maps, safety assessments,
current hazard reports, or guarantees of access. Source data may be incomplete.
